# Personal site

José Viloria’s static personal site: a resume at `/` and a Blog of Posts.

## Language

**Post**:
A dated piece of writing on the Blog, identified by a slug and optional Tags.
_Avoid_: Article, entry, page

**Draft**:
A Post that exists in the source but is not published for readers.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, hidden, private

**Tag**:
A free-form label that groups related Posts for browsing.
_Avoid_: Category, topic, keyword

**Blog**:
The area of the site where Posts are listed, read, and discovered (including Tags and the feed).
_Avoid_: Journal, news, articles section
